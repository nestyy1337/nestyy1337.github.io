mail-stuff is a personal tool I wrote for my own mailbox. It reads my Gmail
with Google's read-only scope (`gmail.readonly`) and turns my threads into
three lists: what I owe someone, what I am waiting on, and what is unclear.

It runs on my own server and is used only by me. It is not offered to anyone
else, and it cannot send, delete, label or archive mail.

[Privacy policy](@/mail-stuff-privacy.md)
